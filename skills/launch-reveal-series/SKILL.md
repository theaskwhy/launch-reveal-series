# Launch Reveal Series

Plan a daily short-video series that reveals one feature at a time before and through a launch. By The Ask Why Company.

## Why it works
Viewers don't know how many features remain, so each episode creates anticipation for the next. **Never show the total** ("Day 1 of 22"). Use "Day N of showing you what we built" and end every episode with "Tomorrow: …".

## Steps
1. **Count what's real.** List features that are actually ON in the build people will get. Exclude anything hidden, unfinished or behind a switch. Combine small features so each episode has one clear "wow".
2. **Shape the run:** Day 1 trailer (a glimpse of the best 3 features) → one feature per day → final day launch ("It's here") — publish "download" only when every store listing is live; prepare a "still in review" version.
3. **Order:** strongest visual features first (week 1 = "show me"), depth in week 2, habit/community/trust in week 3, invite/share just before launch.
4. **Per episode (15–30 s):** 0–2 s hook (real product on screen or a strong real-world clip) → series line → one feature, one idea, shown by a **real screen recording** → "Tomorrow: …" → one call to action (waitlist or download).
5. **Captions:** one plain sentence on what it does + one specific question + up to 3 hashtags. Searchable words in caption and on-screen text (search drives much short-form discovery).
6. **Per platform:** same master video, separate caption, cover and sound choice. Read each account's own insights for active times before fixing post times. Brand accounts on TikTok use the Commercial Music Library.
7. **Production:** record all screen captures in one session from the release build (Do Not Disturb, sample data, no personal info), batch-edit from one template, watch every export muted and with sound.
8. **Schedule free:** native schedulers (Meta Business Suite for Instagram/Facebook, TikTok web scheduler in ~10-day batches, YouTube Studio). Collab posts usually can't be scheduled — post those by hand.
9. **Warm the accounts first** if they've been quiet: a few normal posts before Day 1 so the series lands on active feeds.
10. **Measure** at 24 h and 7 d: watch time, completion, shares/sends per reach, saves, follows, link taps, sign-ups per 1,000 reached. Change one thing at a time.

## Rules
- No fake UI, no mock data presented as live, no promised dates before store approval.
- AI-generated video only for labelled openers, never as product proof.
- Show safety and privacy features honestly (age rules, reporting) — it builds trust with parents and reviewers.
